    FAQ

    Questions we often get

    Have another question? Message us on WhatsApp and we'll get back to you.

    I don't have an IT team. Can you be my IT department?

    Yes, it's one of our core services. We run an assessment, build a work plan and manage your company's technology as if we were part of your team.

    Do you work with small businesses?

    Yes. We adapt our service to the size and needs of each company, from a shop with a few computers to organizations with several branches.

    How does support work?

    We handle your users' issues remotely or on site, as needed, and keep a record to prevent problems from happening again.

    Can you help me lower my phone costs?

    Yes. We analyze your landline and mobile usage, advise you on technologies such as IP telephony and work with agreements with the main carriers.

    How much does it cost?

    It depends on what you need. The first meeting is free: we get to know your company and then send you a proposal with a clear scope and cost.

    What is Felizia?

    It's the artificial intelligence division of Grupo Tecnolakis. It helps small and medium businesses automate tasks, add AI assistants and train their teams. Learn more at felizia.com.ar.
